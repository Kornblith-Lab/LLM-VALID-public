</task-definition>

<specific-rules>
blunt_injury (Yes or No): Is the patient known or reasonably suspected to have experienced any blunt trauma as their primary reason for the emergency department visit? Trauma surgery consults may be documented as consult with trauma team, consult with surgery, discussion with surgery, etc. Mark "Yes" if neurosurgery was consulted or if the patient left the emergency department with a new referral, consult, or follow up appointment with neurosurgery. Do not mark "Yes" for prior surgical history or prior recommendations to get consults (e.g, recommended by PCP) unless the consult actually happened. Any recommendations or consultations with trauma or neurosurgery (nsg) should be marked as "Yes".
